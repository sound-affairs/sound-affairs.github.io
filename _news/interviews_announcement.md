Sound Affairs is launching a new interview series with scholars working in different fields and disciplines, aiming to cover the latest developments in Sound Studies. 

What drives sonic research today? Which questions are worth asking (again)? What have we missed? Which histories are being uncovered? And how does this research possibly impact society? 

These and other questions we will discuss in different installments, beginning with an interview with Holger Schulze (University of Copenhagen), which will be posted in the coming week. 

Stay tuned!
